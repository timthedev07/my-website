# Focus and Aim

The primary aim of this CAS activity is to enhance communication skills within the realm of mathematics. Oftentimes, phrasing ideas clearly and cohesively requires a lot of practice and yet is an important skill in scientific research. Taking advantage of my passion for mathematics, I will create a blog where I explain my solutions to and thought processes in mathematical olympiad problems. To further prepare for future mathematical research, I will build the entire website in German. This will help to learn subject-specific vocabulary, which will provide me with the skills to comprehend mathematical essays, publications, and works in German, which appears a lot in research papers.

# CAS Stages

## Investigation & Preparation

Every month, I will find a math olympiad past question, go through it in detail, and prepare all the necessary bits to compose a full proof.

## Action

Write up the proofs in German, with clear diagrams and the best possible grammar. I will collaborate with my colleagues in the German class to peer-assess the use of language. By the end of the process, I will publish the article on [my website](https://mathe-auf-deutsch.vercel.app/).
