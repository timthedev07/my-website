# Focus and Aim

In response to and building on top of the thought-provoking topic of identity brought up in the Lang B course, I decided to write and publish an article on my own Next.js website to deepen my understanding of the German language and engage with the globally recurring topic of identity and life philosophies.

# Learning Outcomes

Addressed Learning Outcomes:

1. Demonstrate engagement with issues of global significance.
2. Demonstrate how to initiate and plan a CAS experience.
3. Recognize and consider the ethics of choices and actions.

# CAS Stages

## Investigation & Preparation

Find a few key questions whose answers can form the basis of the article, plan how the questions are going to be responded to, and determine the structure of the article.

## Action

Write the article in .mdx in German, with appropriate textual and graphical elements to support my arguments.

Then, using next-mdx-remote, publish the article on a page on my website.
